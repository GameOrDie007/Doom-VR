Doom VR - Doom, Doom II, Final Doom, Doom 64, Heretic, Hexen, Strife and
hundreds of megawads and mods, in PC VR.            (version: see version.txt)
==========================================================================

A Game Or Die VR port: UZDoom 5.0.1 with a VR mode built in, over OpenXR.
This download has no games in it. Setup.bat finds the ones you own.

NEEDS
    Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a PC VR headset with an OpenXR runtime
    (SteamVR, Virtual Desktop or Meta Quest Link), and a graphics card that
    handles PC VR. Your own copies of the commercial games; the free games
    and the megawads need nothing.

INSTALL
    1. Extract the zip anywhere, then double-click Setup.bat in the
       extracted folder (not from inside the zip).
    2. Setup makes a VR folder for Doom VR: inside your DOOM + DOOM II folder
       (Steam or GOG) when you have it, otherwise Documents\Doom VR. Then it:
         - finds your Doom, Doom II, Final Doom, Master Levels, Doom 64,
           Heretic + Hexen, Strife and Chex games on Steam and GOG (own one
           somewhere else? drop its folder onto Setup.bat) and copies them in;
         - downloads the free games and mods: Freedoom, Blasphemer, the
           classic megawads, Beautiful Doom, Voxel Doom II, NashGore, Brutal
           Doom, Project Brutality, DOOM CE, the total conversions and more.
           The first time that is about 5.5 GB, so allow about 6 minutes on
           a fast connection and longer on a slow one. Setup says what it is
           downloading as it goes;
         - makes Doom 64's IWAD and its Lost Levels from your own DOOM64.WAD,
           a cover for every game from its own title screen, and a launcher
           per game.
       It also offers a desktop shortcut and an entry in your Steam library.
       Run it again any time: it only adds, and never touches your saves or
       settings. What it did is in setup-log.txt in the VR folder.
    3. Start your VR runtime, then the Doom VR shortcut (or Doom VR.cmd in the
       VR folder). "Doom VR (flat).cmd" plays on the monitor with no headset.
    Bought another Doom-family game later? Run "Find my new games.cmd" in
    the VR folder. To remove Doom VR, run Uninstall.bat there: your settings
    and saved games are kept, and your games themselves are never touched.

    UPDATING keeps your saved games, your settings, your weapon and hand
    fits and your notes in the Game Tome: run the new version's Setup.bat
    (or update in the Game Or Die Installer).
    Settings acting up? "Reset settings.cmd" in the VR folder puts them back
    to how they came (the old ones are kept beside them); saved games, fits
    and notes stay.

TESTED ON
    Meta Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop, on an RTX 3080 Ti and an RTX 5080, at
    90 frames a second; the Steam Frame through SteamVR. Valve Index, Vive
    and Windows Mixed Reality controllers are supported but not yet tested
    by us.

FAQ
    Is it free? Yes, always.
    Do I need my own games? For Doom, Doom II, Final Doom, Doom 64, Heretic,
        Hexen and Strife, yes: Steam or GOG, the classic releases and the
        re-releases alike. The free games and the megawads need nothing.
    Which headsets? Any PC VR headset with an OpenXR runtime: Virtual
        Desktop, Steam Link, Quest Link or Air Link, Index, Vive, Windows
        Mixed Reality, and the Steam Frame through SteamVR.
    Standalone on a Quest 3 or the Steam Frame? Not yet.
    Multiplayer? Single player for now.
    Are my saves safe? Yes, and updating keeps them (see UPDATING).
    What PC do I need? One that runs PC VR well. On a weaker PC, set Weapon
        detail to Standard (VR Options > Hands and Weapons) and play with the
        Classic style instead of Modern VR.
    Brutal Doom, Project Brutality, Beautiful Doom, Voxel Doom, DOOM CE?
        Yes: Setup fetches them and Switch Game has them, our 3D guns
        included. A mod with its own weapon art shows that art.
    Left-handed? Seated? Yes: VR Options > Hands and Weapons > Leftorium
        (left-handed), and recenter seated; Physical Play > Seated reach
        lifts the holsters.
    It started on the monitor, not in the headset: start your VR runtime
        first, then Doom VR. Still flat, or something broke? See IF
        SOMETHING GOES WRONG below.

PLAYING
    The Doom VR shortcut opens the game picker on Doom: every game, grouped,
    with its cover. Pick one and it loads in a second or two without leaving
    the headset. The same list is Switch Game in the main menu, any time.
    STYLE: Modern VR adds what fits each game - Voxel Doom, NashGore and its
    voxel gore, upscaled textures, HD sounds, remastered music. The shortcut
    always starts in Modern VR. Classic is the game as it was made, for the
    session: bottom of Switch Game, then Restart this game.
    BUILD YOUR OWN GAME: a game plus maps, one gameplay mod, looks, sounds
    and music. What does not fit your picks is gray - highlight it to see why.
    YOUR OWN ART: put 600 x 900 covers named like the game ('Doom II.png') in
    the VR folder's game\boxart\, and a 1920 x 1080
    game\boxart\graphics\vrsplash.png, then run "Find my new games.cmd".

CONTROLS
    Left stick ........ walk (where you look); hold it in to run
    Right stick ....... turn; flick up = use item, down = next item
    Right trigger ..... fire (where your hand points; the red dot shows where
                        the shot will land); clicks in menus
    Left trigger ...... alt-fire, or the weapon in your left hand (VR mods)
    A ................. use / doors                         B ... crouch
    X ................. reload                              Y ... jump
    Grips ............. tap: previous / next weapon
    Right stick, held in  weapon wheel: your guns around your hand, the game
                        slowed; move your hand to one and let go
    Both grips held ... recenter (in menus too: it puts the menu back in
                        front of you)
    Both palms up ..... hands together, palms up as if holding a book, for a
                        second: the Game Tome, the game's manual as a book
                        (also in VR Options); B, Y or Menu closes it
    Left menu button .. menu
    In any menu, HOLD the right grip and point your right controller at the
    screen - a dot shows where - and pull the trigger to click. Let go and the
    sticks and buttons have the menu; a quick tap of the grip pages down.
    PHOTO MODE: in the menu, click the LEFT STICK to hide it and look around
    the frozen world; any other button brings the menu back.
    To quit: menu, Quit Game, A, A.

VR OPTIONS (first in Options)
    Recenter, Switch Game, Build Your Own Game and the style, then:
    Turning and Moving ... snap or smooth turning, walking direction and
                           speed, head bob, comfort vignette (off to start)
    Your Body and Room ... eye height; walk around your room; crouch for
                           real; fade to black when your head is in a wall
    Hands and Weapons .... THE LEFTORIUM (left-handed: the gun in your left
                           hand, the left trigger fires; you still walk with
                           the left stick - "Swap sticks" swaps them); weapon
                           size; tune the weapon in your hand; red dot on or
                           off, its size and opacity; vibration and its
                           strength; your own hands (holding each gun);
                           weapon detail, Standard or Fine (from the next
                           game)
    Physical Play ........ play with your body - see PHYSICAL PLAY below
    HUD and Menu Screen .. HUD and menu screen size and distance; pointing at
                           menus; the steady desktop view
    Seated or standing, recenter the way you will play: wherever your eyes
    are then becomes Doom's eye height.

PHYSICAL PLAY (VR Options > Physical Play; each part on or off, or all at
once with "Physical" and "Simple"). The buttons always keep working.
    Holsters ......... two, on your gun hand's side: the last one-handed
                       gun you used is on your hip, the last two-handed gun
                       on top of your shoulder. Reach there and squeeze grip
                       to draw it; squeeze there again to put it back. Both
                       guns are drawn on you; a buzz, a ring and the gun's
                       name show a holster in reach. Every other weapon is
                       in the weapon wheel. Seated, "Seated reach" lifts them.
    Swing to strike .. fists, chainsaw, staffs, every melee weapon
    Two hands ........ long guns: hold the other hand's grip on the gun's
                       front and aim from hand to hand; "Two-handed aim:
                       Gun stock" aims from your face through the gun hand,
                       as along a rifle's stock, with the gun raised
                       (while you hold a long gun, that grip held never
                       changes weapon; a quick tap still does)
    Press by hand .... push switches and doors with either hand
    Items at mouth ... Heretic, Hexen, Strife: the other hand to your mouth,
                       grip, and the selected item is used
    Wrist ............ health, armor and the face on the end of the other
                       wrist, ammo on the gun hand's (or a watch on the
                       back of the wrist: VR Options)

STEAM FRAME (and any SteamVR headset)
    Start SteamVR first, then DoomVR.bat on the PC (or from SteamVR's
    desktop view in the headset). While SteamVR is running, Doom VR uses it
    for that game, whatever Windows' VR setting says, and changes nothing
    on your PC. Started from the Frame's own library, it plays flat.
    The Frame's buttons follow the official Steam releases' gamepad layouts,
    with the motion controllers aiming and the triggers firing as usual:
                     Doom games             Heretic, Hexen, Strife
      A              use                    jump
      B              crouch                 run
      X              reload                 use
      Y              automap                automap
      LB / RB        previous / next weapon, in both
      D-pad          weapons 2 to 5         fly up and down, pick an item
      Left stick     run (click)            crouch (click)
      Right stick    zoom (click)           use the item (click)
      View / Menu    scores / menu, in both
    On the automap the D-pad pans and LB / RB zoom. Hold both grips to
    recenter. Each is a key you can rebind (VRF_A, VRF_DPadUp...).

STREAMING AND RECORDING
    The monitor shows the observer view: steady, following your turns but
    not every small head movement, with the horizon kept level, and with the
    menus, messages and status bar over it as you see them. HUD and Menu
    Screen > Steady desktop view turns the steadying off.

KNOWN ISSUES
    - Room-scale and crouching for real work in single player only.
    - Mods made for other VR ports load, but their guns may sit differently
      in the hand; the weapon tuner fits each one and remembers it.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
    Run "Collect logs.bat" in the VR folder. It puts the logs, the settings
    and a short report on this PC into one zip on your desktop: send that
    with your report. Nothing is uploaded and no game files are copied.
