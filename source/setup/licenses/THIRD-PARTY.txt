Doom VR - what is in this folder, and under which licence
=========================================================

Doom VR itself (uzdoom.exe, uzdoom.pk3 and the other .pk3 files beside it) is
UZDoom 5.0.1 with a VR mode added, and is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later, like
UZDoom. The source is at https://github.com/GameOrDie007/Doom-VR.

Game Or Die's own work in this download - the Game Tome (gametome\: the
layer and its books), Game Or Die Hands (setup\gdh\: the hands, their
styles, gdh_setup.exe and the 3D gun packs), the covers, splash and
backdrop (setup\boxart\, setup\*.png) and the Setup scripts (setup\*.ps1,
the .bat files) - is Copyright (C) 2026 Game Or Die, all rights reserved.
It may be redistributed only as part of Doom VR, unchanged. It is not
covered by the GPL: it is not built into uzdoom.exe. The CC0 work it is
made from is credited below. The Game Or Die name and logo are not licensed
for any other use.

Built into uzdoom.exe:

  UZDoom 5.0.1 and the libraries it builds with
      GPL-3.0-or-later; each component's own licence is in licenses.zip,
      which comes unchanged from UZDoom's 5.0.1 Windows release.
      abseil and cppdap, two of those libraries, are Apache-2.0: the text
      is licenses\Apache-2.0.txt.
  pcvr-xr, Game Or Die's OpenXR layer
      GPL-3.0-or-later - licenses\pcvr-xr (GPL-3.0).txt. Its source is
      published with Doom VR's.

Beside uzdoom.exe:

  openxr_loader.dll    the Khronos OpenXR SDK 1.0.25 loader, unchanged.
                       Apache-2.0, Copyright (c) 2017-2023 The Khronos
                       Group Inc. - licenses\Apache-2.0.txt.
                       https://github.com/KhronosGroup/OpenXR-SDK
  soft_oal.dll         OpenAL Soft, LGPL - from UZDoom's 5.0.1 release,
                       with its licence in licenses.zip.
  sndfile.dll          libsndfile, LGPL - as above.
  soundfonts\, fm_banks\
                       UZDoom's music banks, from the same release.

In setup\gdh\:

  gdh_setup.exe, hand_r.gdh, hand_l.gdh, hand_ao.tga
                       Game Or Die Hands - our VR hands and the tool that
                       fits them to each game at setup. Game Or Die's own
                       work; the hand models are made from the Godot XR
                       Tools hands by DigitalN8m4r3 (CC0 1.0, public
                       domain). The hand skins and held weapons are made by
                       SETUP.bat on your PC from your own games; none of
                       that game art is in this folder.

  slayer\, slayer_armor\
                       The Doom games' hand styles (VR Options > Hands and
                       Weapons > Hands style): the classic marine's padded
                       gloves and the Slayer's armored gloves. Game Or Die's
                       own work on the same CC0 hands; their textures use
                       three CC0 1.0 (public domain) photo scans: "Human
                       Face Skin Texture" (Skin 0001) from TextureCan.com,
                       and Leather 014 and Fabric 045 from ambientCG.com.

  gameordie\
                       The Game Or Die gloves (Hands style: Game Or Die).
                       Game Or Die's own work on the same CC0 hands.

  doomvr-weapons-doom-b.pk3
                       Game Or Die's own Doom guns, copied into mods\ by
                       SETUP.bat. Our own models and textures, made by
                       script; the pistol, shotgun and super shotgun are
                       built on Quaternius's LowPoly guns and the chainsaw
                       on loafbrr's "Chainsaw", both CC0 1.0 (public
                       domain) - thank you, Quaternius and loafbrr. No game
                       art is in it.

Not in this folder:

  No game data. SETUP.bat copies your own games in from Steam, GOG or a
  folder you point it at, and fetches the free games and mods from where
  their authors publish them. Each keeps its own licence and credits.
